# BANKING77 data card

Source: https://github.com/PolyAI-LDN/task-specific-datasets/tree/master/banking_data

License: [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/PolyAI-LDN/task-specific-datasets/blob/57ec275d8078af65b7731c2a98be812d844a6d6b/LICENSE).
Credit: Iñigo Casanueva, Tadas Temcinas, Daniela Gerz, Matthew Henderson and Ivan Vulić,
*Efficient Intent Detection with Dual Sentence Encoders*, NLP for ConvAI, ACL 2020,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04807. Changes: validation, deduplication and new development splits.

Public English benchmark of online banking queries, 77 single-label intents.
Upstream reports 10,003 train and 3,080 test rows. Actual audited counts will be
generated by the data command. Fields: text and category. No priority, user,
conversation or timestamp fields. No private service data are used. Public
availability does not guarantee all content is non-sensitive; a pattern screen
and manual sample review will be documented. Raw data are not committed.

Limitations: English only; banking only; single intent per utterance; no real
deployment distribution, OOD labels or priority ground truth. Benchmark accuracy
does not establish safe automatic handling of financial disputes.
